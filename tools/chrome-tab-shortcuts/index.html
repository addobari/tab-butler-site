Chrome tab shortcuts: a one-page cheat sheet

Every keyboard shortcut for tabs and windows in Chrome, for Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS, on one page you can print.

One page, both keyboards. Checked in Chrome, November 2026.

Short answer

The ones worth learning first: Ctrl+Shift+T reopens the tab or window you just closed, Ctrl+Shift+A searches every open tab, Ctrl+1 to 8 jumps to a tab by its place, and Ctrl+Shift+D bookmarks every tab in the window. On a Mac, use ⌘ in place of Ctrl.

Search from the address bar

  • Type @tabs and a space, then a word, to switch to a matching open tab.
  • Type @bookmarks or @history and a space to search those.
  • With Simmer, type sm and a space to search open tabs, tabs you put away and bookmarks together.

Questions

What is the shortcut to reopen a closed tab in Chrome?
Ctrl+Shift+T on Windows, Linux and ChromeOS, and ⌘+Shift+T on a Mac. Press it again to reopen the tab closed before that. If you closed a window, it comes back with all its tabs.
How do I switch tabs with the keyboard in Chrome?
Ctrl+Tab goes to the next tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab to the previous one, on every computer. Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 (⌘+1 to ⌘+8 on a Mac) jump to a tab by its place, and Ctrl+9 (⌘+9) to the last.
How do I search my open tabs in Chrome?
Press Ctrl+Shift+A (⌘+Shift+A on a Mac), or type @tabs and a space in the address bar.
Can I change Chrome's keyboard shortcuts?
Chrome's own shortcuts can't be changed. Shortcuts for extensions can be set at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
