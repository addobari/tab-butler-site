Free tools

Small tools for tabs and links. They run in this page: no sign-up, no trackers, and nothing you paste is sent anywhere.

  • Open a list of links in tabs, all at once

    Paste links, or any text with links in it, and open them all in new tabs. Duplicates and tracking tags are taken out first. Nothing leaves your browser.

  • Turn a list of links into Chrome bookmarks

    Paste links or a OneTab export and download a bookmarks file Chrome can import, sorted into a folder per site, or copy them as Markdown. Nothing leaves your browser.

  • Chrome tab shortcuts: a one-page cheat sheet

    Every keyboard shortcut for tabs and windows in Chrome, for Windows, Mac, Linux and ChromeOS, on one page you can print.
