Open a list of links in tabs, all at once

Paste links, or any text with links in it, and open them all in new tabs. Duplicates and tracking tags are taken out first. Nothing leaves your browser.

Paste links to begin.

Runs in this page. What you paste isn't sent anywhere or saved.

Short answer

Paste your links in the box and press Open. The tool finds every web address in what you paste, skips duplicates, takes out tracking tags, and opens the links in new tabs, ten at a time. If Chrome opens only one, click the blocked pop-up icon at the end of the address bar, choose to always allow pop-ups from this site, and press Open again.

What it does with your links

  • Finds the links. Paste a list, an email, a document or a OneTab export: every address that starts with http:// or https:// is picked out, and the words around it are left behind.
  • Skips duplicates. The same page twice opens once.
  • Takes out tracking tags. Tags such as utm_source, fbclid and gclid are removed. They tell a site where you came from and don't change the page.
  • Opens ten at a time. Press Open again for the next ten, so a long list doesn't swamp the window.

If only one link opens

Chrome blocks a page from opening many tabs at once unless you allow it. After the first press, look for the blocked pop-up icon at the right end of the address bar, choose to always allow pop-ups and redirects from simmer.addobari.com, then press Open again. You can take the permission back at any time in Chrome's site settings.

Keep the set together next time

A list of links is often a task you put down: a trip, a purchase, a bug. Simmer, our free Chrome extension, keeps those sets for you. It sorts your open tabs into the things you're in the middle of, puts a whole task away when you're done for now, and brings every tab back in one click. To keep this list as bookmarks instead, use Links to bookmarks.

Questions

Why does only one link open?
Chrome's pop-up blocker allows one new tab per click. Click the blocked pop-up icon at the end of the address bar, choose to always allow pop-ups from this site, and press Open again.
Is there a limit to how many links I can open?
The tool opens ten at a time; press Open again for the next ten. Opening hundreds of tabs at once can slow Chrome down, so it's better in batches.
Does this tool see or keep my links?
No. It runs in the page, in your browser. The links aren't sent anywhere or saved, and the page has no trackers.
Can I paste a OneTab export?
Yes. OneTab's export puts one link and its title on each line; the tool picks out the links and opens them.
